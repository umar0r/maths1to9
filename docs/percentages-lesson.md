# Percentages of amounts

Folder: `content/percentages-of-amounts/`. Uses the significant figures lesson's four-stage layout, shared scoring and hash router, saved progress, guided checks, practice review and final assessment. The footer has one action, labelled Continue or Check answer. The final Continue returns to the lesson catalogue.

Scope: parts per hundred, common fraction/decimal equivalents, percentages of amounts using fractions, 10% and 1%, decimal multipliers, and amounts above 100%. Includes a keyboard-accessible 20-sector colouring activity, eight learning slides, four three-step examples, twelve practice questions and eight final-check questions.

References: user-provided lesson text and screenshots, with original interactive graphics and worked examples. The initial HEIC could not be decoded. The subsequently supplied readable photograph of “Proportions of amounts” informed the fraction method, efficient percentage building blocks and comparison guidance. Textbook examples were not copied. Existing slide IDs and question positions were retained to preserve saved progress.

Curriculum checked against AQA R9 and R16:
https://www.aqa.org.uk/subjects/mathematics/gcse/mathematics-8300/specification/subject-content/3.3-ratio-proportion-and-rates-of-change

Later lessons remain separate: expressing one amount as a percentage of another; increase/decrease; reverse percentages; simple interest; repeated change and compound interest. AQA places compound interest in additional foundation content and general iterative processes in higher content.

Validation: `node tests/percentages.cjs`. Browser walkthrough also checked full progression, incorrect retries, circle selection, guided steps, assessment results, score persistence, deep links, single footer action and mobile overflow.
